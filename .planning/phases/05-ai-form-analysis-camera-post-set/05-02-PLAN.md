    **1. Conventions.** BlazePose indices (0 nose, 9/10 mouth, 11/12 shoulders, 13/14 elbows, 15/16 wrists, 23/24 hips, 25/26 knees, 27/28 ankles, 29/30 heels, 31/32 foot index). Coordinates isotropic: x multiplied by aspectRatio before geometry. Angles are 2D at the middle joint. Torso = shoulder-to-hip distance; L = shoulder-to-ankle distance; thigh = hip-to-knee distance. Side-on exercises use the near side (chain with higher mean visibility). `ramp(v, good, poor)` = 100 at good, 0 at poor, linear between, clamped; NaN -> feature not available (excluded from the mean).

    **2. Camera views and assessability (D-17, D-18).** push_up side/landscape: ROM yes, CORE yes, SYMMETRY "Not assessed from this angle". dip side/landscape: same. squat side/portrait: same. pull_up front-or-back/portrait: ROM yes, CORE yes (low confidence, lateral sway + tempo only), SYMMETRY yes.

    **3. Rep segmentation (per exercise).** Primary signal and rest level: push_up elbow angle (rest high, min amplitude 40 deg); dip elbow angle (rest high, 40 deg); squat knee angle (rest high, 30 deg), fallback when near-knee mean visibility < 0.6: `-hipY / thigh` (rest high, amplitude 0.5 thigh); pull_up clearance `(barY - mouthY) / torso` where barY = median wrist y over the set (rest low, amplitude 0.5 torso). Hysteresis on smoothed signal: amp = p95 - p5; upper = p5 + 0.7 amp; lower = p5 + 0.3 amp; a rep is rest -> past the far threshold -> back to rest. Keep reps with duration 0.8-15 s and >= 60% valid frames; others are dropped and counted. Minimum reps for a band: **2** (fewer -> UNTRACKABLE `NO_REPS_DETECTED`).

    **4. Per-exercise tags.** Table columns: tag, dimension, per-rep feature, MEDIUM when, HIGH when, label, cue.
    push_up:
    - PU_SHALLOW_DEPTH | ROM | minElbowDeg | > 100 | > 120 | "Shallow depth" | "Lower until your chest is a fist's height from the floor."

    - PU_HIP_SAG | CORE | meanHipOffset (L units, + = below line) | > 0.06 | > 0.12 | "Hips sagging" | "Squeeze your glutes and brace your abs to keep one straight line from head to heels."
    - PU_HIP_PIKE | CORE | -meanHipOffset | > 0.08 | never | "Hips piking up" | "Lower your hips so your body forms one straight line."
    - PU_RUSHED_TEMPO | CORE | durationSec | LOW when < 1.0 | never | "Rushed reps" | "Take about a second on the way down and control the push."
    pull_up:
    - PL_CHIN_NOT_OVER_BAR | ROM | mouthBelowBar (torso units at rep peak, + = below bar) | > 0.03 | > 0.15 | "Chin not clearing the bar" | "Pull until your chin passes over the bar."
    - PL_NO_FULL_HANG | ROM | maxElbowDeg (mean of both arms) | < 155 | never | "Not lowering all the way" | "Lower until your arms are straight before the next rep."
    - PL_ASYM_PULL | SYMMETRY | meanElbowDiffDeg or maxShoulderTiltDeg | > 12 or > 8 | > 20 or > 14 | "Pulling unevenly" | "Pull with both arms equally and keep your shoulders level."
    - PL_RUSHED_TEMPO | CORE | durationSec | LOW when < 1.2 | never | "Rushed reps" | "Lower yourself slowly instead of dropping."
    dip:
    - DIP_SHALLOW_DEPTH | ROM | minElbowDeg | > 110 | > 125 | "Shallow depth" | "Lower until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor."
    - DIP_EXCESSIVE_DEPTH | ROM | minElbowDeg | LOW when < 70 (never higher) | never | "Going very deep" | "Stop around upper arms parallel to the floor unless you've built up to deeper dips."
    - DIP_INCOMPLETE_LOCKOUT | ROM | maxElbowDeg | < 155 | never | "Arms not fully straight at the top" | "Press up until your arms are straight."
    - DIP_EXCESSIVE_LEAN | CORE | trunkLeanDeg at bottom | > 45 | never | "Leaning far forward" | "Keep your chest more upright as you lower."
    - DIP_SWING | CORE | hipSwayRatio (hip x range / torso) | > 0.25 | never | "Swinging" | "Keep your legs still and move only up and down."
    squat:
    - SQ_SHALLOW_DEPTH | ROM | hipBelowKnee (thigh units, + = hip below knee) or minKneeDeg | < -0.05 or > 110 | < -0.20 | "Shallow depth" | "Sit down until your hips reach knee height."
    - SQ_EXCESSIVE_TRUNK_LEAN | CORE | trunkLeanDeg - shinLeanDeg, trunkLeanDeg | > 25 or trunk > 60 | never | "Leaning forward a lot" | "Keep your chest up and sit your hips straight down."

    - SQ_INCOMPLETE_STAND | ROM | maxKneeDeg | LOW when < 160 | never | "Not standing all the way up" | "Stand tall at the top of every rep."
    - SQ_RUSHED_TEMPO | CORE | durationSec | LOW when < 1.0 | never | "Bouncing through reps" | "Control the way down and pause briefly at the bottom."

All: injury, pain, medical terms, height, weight, body composition.

Scoring ramps (per rep -> dimension).** push_up ROM = mean(ramp(minElbowDeg, 90, 130), ramp(maxElbowDeg, 165, 140)); CORE = 0.85 x bodyLine + 0.15 x ramp(durationSec, 1.5, 0.6), bodyLine = ramp(meanHipOffset, 0.03, 0.15) if offset >= 0 else ramp(-offset, 0.04, 0.16). dip ROM = mean(ramp(minElbowDeg, 95, 130), ramp(maxElbowDeg, 165, 140)); CORE = 0.6 x ramp(trunkLeanDeg, 30, 60) + 0.4 x ramp(hipSwayRatio, 0.10, 0.35). squat ROM = mean(ramp(hipBelowKnee, 0.0, -0.30), ramp(maxKneeDeg, 170, 150)); CORE = 0.7 x ramp(trunkLeanDeg - shinLeanDeg, 15, 40) + 0.3 x ramp(heelRiseDeg, 3, 15). pull_up ROM = mean(ramp(mouthBelowBar, -0.05, 0.25), ramp(maxElbowDeg, 165, 140)); CORE = 0.7 x ramp(hipSwayRatio, 0.10, 0.40) + 0.3 x ramp(durationSec, 1.8, 0.8); SYMMETRY = mean(ramp(meanElbowDiffDeg, 5, 25), ramp(maxShoulderTiltDeg, 4, 15)).
    Set dimension score = round(0.85 x mean(rep scores) + 0.15 x min(rep scores)). Overall = weighted mean over assessed dimensions, renormalised: push_up/dip/squat ROM 0.5, CORE 0.5; pull_up ROM 0.5, CORE 0.1, SYMMETRY 0.4 (planner proposal: RESEARCH used 0.4/0.4/0.2, lowered pull-up CORE because it is low confidence from the front).

    **7. Bands (D-05, D-15, level-agnostic).** >= 90 Excellent, 75-89 Good, 60-74 Fair, 40-59 Needs work, < 40 Poor. Dimension chips use the same cutoffs. Score is rounded to an integer before banding.

    **8. Finding selection (server-side, deterministic, FORM-07).** A rep tag is set-level detected when affected reps >= max(1, ceil(0.4 x repCount)); set severity = highest severity among affected reps; weight LOW 1, MEDIUM 2, HIGH 3; rank = weight x affectedFraction, ties by dimension order ROM, CORE, SYMMETRY then tag code. Keep at most 2 corrections, at most one per dimension unless only one dimension has detected tags. Positives (max 3, in this order, each with rep numbers): assessed dimension at Good or better; full lockout/stand on every rep; consistent depth (std of depth feature: push_up/dip minElbowDeg < 8 deg, squat hipBelowKnee < 0.05, pull_up mouthBelowBar < 0.05); steady tempo (no rushed tag and duration coefficient of variation < 0.25); always-last fallback "You finished N reps".

    **9. Trackability gate (FORM-09, D-12).** Over the exercise's key joints: TOO_FEW_FRAMES if frames < 15 or duration < 3 s; LOW_CONFIDENCE if mean visibility < 0.5; KEY_JOINTS_HIDDEN if key joints visible (>= 0.5) in < 70% of frames; OUT_OF_FRAME if > 10% of key-joint coordinates fall outside [0, 1]; TOO_FAR if median torso length < 0.12 of frame height; WRONG_VIEW if (side exercises) median shoulder width / torso > 0.55 or (pull_up) < 0.25; NO_REPS_DETECTED if fewer than 2 reps. Untrackable sets get no band, no LLM call, and the session still completes.

    **10. Live setup check before recording (D-03, D-04).** Window = last 15 frames (about 1 s at 15 fps). Ready when key joints of the visible side have visibility >= 0.5 in >= 80% of frames, landmark bounding box within [0.02, 0.98] on both axes, and body long axis spans >= 40% of the frame, held continuously for 1 s. Countdown 8 s (inside D-03's 5-10 s); pauses with "Step back into frame" if the check fails during the countdown. Recording auto-stops at 175 s (capture cap 180 s).
